# Key Business Insights

- Technology category generated the highest sales and profit.
- Furniture category showed low profitability.
- West region contributed the highest revenue.
- Consumer segment was the top-performing customer segment.
- Phones, Chairs, and Storage were the top-selling products.

---

# Statistical Validation

## Business Question
Is the Technology category significantly more profitable than the Furniture category?

## Hypothesis Testing

### Null Hypothesis (H₀)
There is no significant difference in average profit between Technology and Furniture categories.

### Alternative Hypothesis (H₁)
Technology category has significantly higher average profit than Furniture category.

## Statistical Test Used
Independent T-Test

## Conclusion
The p-value obtained from the T-test was less than 0.05, indicating that the Technology category is significantly more profitable than Furniture.
